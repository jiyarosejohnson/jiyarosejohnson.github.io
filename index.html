About Me

I am a Research Associate at Indian Institute of Technology Madras .

I completed my PhD in Mathematics at Indian Institute of Technology Madras under the supervision of Dr. T. V. Anoop .

You can find my full CV here.

My research interests lie in potential theory, analytic PDEs, spectral theory, and geometric analysis. During my PhD, I focused on logarithmic potential operators, and I am interested in further pursuing this line of research. I am also interested in exploring other areas of analysis.
