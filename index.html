About Me

I am a Research Associate at Indian Institute of Technology, Madras .

I completed my PhD in Mathematics at Indian Institute of Technology, Madras under the supervision of Dr. T. V. Anoop .

You can find my full CV here.

My research interests lie in potential theory, analytic PDEs, and spectral theory. During my PhD, I focused on logarithmic potential operators, and I am interested in further pursuing this line of research. I am also interested in elliptic PDEs and am open to exploring other areas of analysis.

Research

My research interests are primarily in algebraic geometry, with a particular focus on deformation theory, toric geometry, polytopes, and matroids.

Algebraic Geometry

General questions in algebraic geometry and related geometric structures.

Deformation Theory

Studying deformations and moduli of algebraic and geometric objects.

Toric Geometry

Toric varieties, combinatorial structures, and their connections with algebraic geometry.

Polytopes & Matroids

Exploring interactions between combinatorics, matroids, polytopes, and algebraic geometry.
